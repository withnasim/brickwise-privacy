Brickwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Brickwise ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game by Naj Studio. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information collected

The app has no sign-up and never asks for your real name, email, contacts, photos or location. It uses Google services that collect some information:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads in the game. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4): helps us understand how the game is played so we can improve it. It collects app-usage events (for example games started, scores and settings used), device and app information, an app-instance ID and, where allowed, the advertising ID.
  • Brickwise leaderboard (Google Firebase): to show the public Top Players list, the app creates an anonymous player ID for your installation (Firebase Authentication) and stores your leaderboard name, your best scores and the time they were posted (Cloud Firestore). The name is chosen at random (for example "LuckyPanda42") and you can change it; other players can see it next to your score. No email, phone number or real name is needed.
  • Google Play Games Services: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your score is also posted to the Brickwise leaderboard in Google Play Games under your Play Games profile.

How information is used

To show the leaderboard, to show ads (personalized or non-personalized, depending on your choices), to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud, to understand how the game is used, and to fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information is processed by Google as described above. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent the first time you play. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are optional: you only see one if you choose to watch it (for example to Continue or to get extra power-ups).
  • You can change your leaderboard name at any time on the home screen. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us; uninstalling the app also ends the link between you and your entry.
  • Gift reminders are local notifications created on your device. You can turn them off in Settings › Gift reminders or in Android's notification settings.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, statistics, settings and current game are saved on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Data retention and deletion

Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs; weekly lists are for that week only. You can delete game data on your device with Settings › Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. To ask about analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new effective date.
